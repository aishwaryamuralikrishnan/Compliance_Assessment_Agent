Compliant and secure

A compliance tool has to practise what it checks. Every statement below can be verified in the public repository: the code, the model configuration and the gateway's documents and settings saved as evidence.

01

Personal data never reaches a model

The input screen redacts e-mail addresses, phone numbers, bank account and card numbers before any model call.

02

Zero data retention at the model providers

The gateway account allows only zero-retention endpoints and no training on requests, and every request carries that policy itself; a reranker that could not meet it was replaced.

03

Data stays in the EU, and not for long

The database is hosted in Frankfurt. Conversations and their saved state are deleted after 30 days by a scheduled job, and a person can have their data deleted on request.

04

Roles and a tool gate

Product manager and DPO see different screens and the agent may call only the tools the signed-in role allows.

05

Guardrails on the way in and out

Prompt injections and off-topic text are refused at the door; every citation in a draft is checked against the law's text, the output gets a disclaimer, and the evaluation caught 14 of 14 planted inputs.

06

Secure sign-in and a public privacy notice

Sign-in through Google with PKCE and a signed eight-hour session. The privacy notice, public in the app, says what is collected, where it goes and how to ask for deletion.
